# Dutch energy taxes and network costs for 2027

> **Status:** The 2027 rates below are based on information supplied on 27 November 2026. Electricity and gas energy-tax rates remain subject to Senate approval. The gas blending obligation may be postponed; if so, its implementation and tariff will also be postponed.

## Energy tax (including VAT)

| Tax | 2026 | 2027 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Gas (per m³) | €0.7268 | €0.7457 | +€0.0189 |
| Electricity, 0–10,000 kWh (per kWh) | €0.1108 | €0.1065 | −€0.0043 |
| Electricity, 10,000–50,000 kWh (per kWh) | €0.0807 | €0.0821 | +€0.0014 |
| Electricity, 50,000–10,000,000 kWh (per kWh) | €0.0452 | €0.0457 | +€0.0005 |
| Energy-tax reduction per electricity connection (per year) | −€628.96 | −€628.69 | €0.27 less reduction |

For household electricity consumption in the first tax bracket, the energy tax decreases by €0.0043/kWh in 2027.

## Green-gas blending obligation (including VAT)

| | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Blending obligation (per m³ of gas) | €0.00 | €0.01815 |

Combined with the increase in gas energy tax, this adds **€0.03705 per m³** in 2027, before any changes to market prices, sourcing fees or network charges. This figure assumes the blending obligation takes effect as announced.

## Network charges

The regional network operators are adjusting their charges. The announcement estimates an increase of approximately **20% for electricity and gas combined** for an average household in 2027. This is an indicative average, not a confirmed increase for an individual connection.

The network operator for the referenced household is **Liander NWN**. Actual 2027 charges depend on the applicable tariff schedule and connection.

## Summary of the stated changes

- Electricity energy tax in the first bracket: **−€0.0043/kWh**.
- Gas energy tax: **+€0.0189/m³**.
- Green-gas blending obligation: **+€0.01815/m³**, subject to implementation.
- Network charges: estimated **approximately +20% combined** for an average household; actual Liander rates may differ.
- Annual electricity energy-tax reduction: **€0.27 lower** in 2027.

These are regulatory and network-charge changes, not changes to the wholesale market price or a supplier's sourcing fee.

## Source and limitations

This summary transcribes and calculates from the rate information supplied in the conversation on 27 November 2026. It is not independently verified against official publications and should be checked against the final government and Liander tariff schedules before use in calculations or customer-facing claims.
