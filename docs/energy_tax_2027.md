# Dutch energy taxes and network costs for 2027

> **Status:** The 2027 rates below are based on information supplied on 27 November 2026 and Frank Energie's article updated 24 November 2026. Rates and proposals remain subject to parliamentary approval and final publication.

## Energy tax (including VAT)

| Tax | 2026 | 2027 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Gas (per m³) | €0.7268 | €0.7457 | +€0.0189 |
| Electricity, 0–10,000 kWh (per kWh) | €0.1108 | €0.1065 | −€0.0043 |
| Electricity, 10,000–50,000 kWh (per kWh) | €0.0807 | €0.0821 | +€0.0014 |
| Electricity, 50,000–10,000,000 kWh (per kWh) | €0.0452 | €0.0457 | +€0.0005 |
| Energy-tax reduction per electricity connection (per year) | −€628.96 | −€628.69 | €0.27 less reduction |

For household electricity consumption in the first tax bracket, the energy tax decreases by approximately €0.00436/kWh in 2027.

## Illustrative tax impact

Frank Energie gives an example for a household using 2,500 kWh electricity and 1,000 m³ gas annually:
- Electricity tax: approximately €10.90 lower per year.
- Gas tax: approximately €18.93 higher per year.
- Net: approximately **€8.03 higher energy tax per year** (around €0.67/month).

This example excludes network costs and market-price changes.

## Green-gas blending obligation (including VAT)

The earlier information supplied in this conversation indicated a blending-obligation charge of €0.01815/m³ in 2027. Frank Energie's article dated 24 November 2026 describes the expected charge as **up to approximately €0.015/m³**. These estimates differ; treat the charge as uncertain until confirmed. The obligation could be postponed.

Using the earlier €0.01815 estimate together with the gas energy-tax increase of €0.0189 would imply a combined increase of **€0.03705/m³**, before market prices, sourcing fees or network charges. This calculation is conditional on that blending charge taking effect.

## Network charges

Frank Energie estimates that an average household's network charges will increase by approximately **€141 per year** in 2027:
- Electricity network charges: approximately **€89/year more**.
- Gas network charges: approximately **€52/year more**.

This is a forecast, not an individual tariff determination. Actual costs depend on the network operator, connection and final tariffs. The network operator for the referenced household is **Liander NWN**.

## ETS2

Frank Energie's article states that ETS2, which would add a carbon-cost mechanism for gas suppliers, has been postponed from 2027 to **2028**. The eventual impact on gas prices was not yet known at the time of the article.

## Summary

- Electricity energy tax in the first bracket: **approximately −€0.00436/kWh**.
- Gas energy tax: **+€0.0189/m³**.
- Green-gas blending obligation: estimate of up to approximately **€0.015/m³** in Frank Energie's article; the previously supplied estimate was €0.01815/m³.
- Network charges: forecast of **€141/year more** for an average household (€89 electricity and €52 gas).
- Annual electricity energy-tax reduction: **€0.27 lower** in 2027.
- ETS2: postponed to 2028 according to the article; its price impact remains uncertain.

These are regulatory and network-charge changes, not changes to wholesale market prices or a supplier's sourcing fee.
